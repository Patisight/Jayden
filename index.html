程锦阳 · 射频 / 天线 / 电磁场。 上海大学通信与信息系统硕士，现任上海利正卫星 EMC 试验工程师； 第一作者发表 IEEE AWPL 论文 1 篇，公开发明专利 3 项。

技术主线两条。其一，车载天线与射频链路的工程化——低剖面 5G MIMO 天线、 多通道集成天线盒、GNSS 有源天线与全向阵列天线，独立完成需求拆解、指标分配、 全波仿真、公差协同与暗室实测。其二，仿真优化自动化——Python 驱动 HFSS 完成参数化建模与多目标寻优（MOPSO / NSGA-II / 贝叶斯），以贝叶斯神经网络代理模型压缩全波仿真次数。

现从事整星与单机 EMC（GJB 151B/C）及磁、力学、真空热环境试验， 参与实验室 CNAS 认可与试验产线建设。

Cheng Jinyang · RF / Antenna / EMC. M.Eng. in Communication and Information Systems, Shanghai University. EMC test engineer in the Shanghai satellite programme; first author of one IEEE AWPL paper, three invention patents published.

Two technical threads. Productisation of vehicular antennas and RF chains — low-profile 5G MIMO antennas, multi-channel integrated antenna boxes, active GNSS and omnidirectional array antennas — with end-to-end ownership of requirement decomposition, spec allocation, full-wave simulation, tolerance review and chamber measurement. Automation of EM optimisation — Python-driven HFSS with multi-objective solvers (MOPSO / NSGA-II / Bayesian), accelerated by BNN surrogate models.

Currently responsible for system- and unit-level EMC (GJB 151B/C) together with magnetic, mechanical and thermal-vacuum qualification, and for CNAS accreditation of the laboratory.
